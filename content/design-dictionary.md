# design-dictionary.org

Working document for the design dictionary site. Sibling of design-books.com
and design-styles.com — same care, its own face.

## Premise

The essential vocabulary of design: 100 terms every working designer should
know across interface, typography, and foundational principles.

## Why this site

Designers argue past each other when the words are fuzzy — hierarchy vs
emphasis, kerning vs tracking, affordance vs signifier. A shared lexicon
makes critique sharper and learning faster.

## Audience

Students, early-career designers, and anyone mid-critique who needs a clean
definition without a textbook chapter.

## Content model

One **term card**:

1. Name
2. One-sentence definition
3. Short practical note
4. Category
5. Related terms

## Seed scope

100 terms across:

- Principles (hierarchy, contrast, balance, proximity…)
- Typography (kerning, leading, x-height, measure…)
- Interface (affordance, Fitts’s Law, progressive disclosure…)
- Layout (grid, gutter, breakpoint, density…)
- Color (hue, value, contrast ratio, palette…)
- Perception (Gestalt, figure–ground, visual flow…)

## Voice

Plain, precise, and short. No jargon for jargon’s sake — define the jargon.
